How the lean is calculated

Each story contributes its bullish and bearish probabilities multiplied by its estimated impact. Impact uses the full distribution: High = 1, Medium = 2/3, Low = 1/3, Cannot tell = 0. The totals are points, not article counts or percentages.

The gauge divides the difference between bullish and bearish totals by total impact weight, then scales it to -100 to +100. Scores from -5 to +5 are balanced. Only displayed bullish or bearish stories contribute; neutral stories and failed calls are excluded. This filtered sample does not represent the full news mix or the odds of a price move.

UNDER THE LENS

01 / RETRIEVE

News, not a crawler

Finnhub supplies headlines and available summaries. We don't scrape publisher pages or claim to read full articles.

02 / DECIDE

Choices, not chat

Foundry scores a fixed set of answers for outlook, impact, and visible sourcing in one API call per story. No generated rationale.

03 / COMPARE

Uncertainty stays visible

Every scored story gets Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral or mixed, with its probability shown. Limited context can count as neutral; errors stay Unscored.
